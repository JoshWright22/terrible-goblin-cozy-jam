# Slush Rush story text. Edit freely; the game reads this file when it starts.
#
# Each day starts with a header line: [campaign day] Title
#   campaign is "summer" (Summer Fun) or "carnival" (Boardwalk Carnival)
# The lines under it are that day's intro card. They're joined into one paragraph, and the text
# shrinks to fit the card, but keep it to about two sentences of story plus the day's rule.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored. A day missing here keeps its built-in text.
#
# [tutorial] holds the Day 1 tutorial steps, one per line, in order. The turn step has a mouse
# version and a touch version split by ||

[summer 1] Opening Day
Uncle Gus left you his beach smoothie shack and fled the country. He left a note telling you the shack was completely legal.
In the back you found two fruit crates.

[summer 2] New Delivery
Blueberries arrived. Nobody ordered them. A note from Uncle Gus assures you there is nothing wrong with them.
A third fruit crate now sits out back.

[summer 3] Odd Shapes
Gus left his "custom" fruit cutter behind. His note says it only cuts shapes he describes as spiritual.
The fruit comes out bent, but you can right click a piece to spin it into place.

[summer 4] Mango Season
Someone carved "trust the mangoes" into the counter. It is Gus's handwriting.
A crate of mangoes turned up this morning, and the customers have noticed.

[summer 5] Lunch Rush
The lifeguards all eat at noon, and every one of them says Gus owes them money.
The ones marked RUSH leave fast but tip double.

[summer 6] Allergy Season
Gus's recipe book has allergy warnings in the margins, mostly written as threats.
Some orders have a crossed out fruit. It stays out of the cup.

[summer 7] Broken Blender
Gus's note on the blenders said hot wash, tumble dry. You followed it.
The blenders shrank to 3x3.

[summer 8] Belt on Overdrive
Another note from Gus: do not press the red button. It has been pressed.
The belt now runs a lot faster than it should.

[summer 9] Rotten Batch
Something was living in the blenders. Gus's notes call it Kevin and ask you not to feed him.
Kevin has moved out, but his rotten fruit is stuck in the blenders.

[summer 10] Heatwave
A postcard from Gus arrived. It says "hot here too." There is no return address.
Fruit left on the belt too long melts away.

[summer 11] Brain Freeze
Gus's note says the freezer runs a little cold. It froze the fruit, the mop and Kevin, who came back.
Frozen pieces won't turn.

[summer 12] Food Critics
A food blogger found the shack and asked about Gus. You said he was on sabbatical.
Customers marked CRITIC want 90% or better, and pay triple when they get it.

[summer 13] Mystery Menu
Customers are vague today. One of them asked if you've seen a man named Gus.
One fruit in every order is a mystery.

[summer 14] Bonfire Night
Someone swears they saw Gus at the bonfire. Then the shack blew a fuse.
The lights are out, so you work by flashlight.

[summer 15] Summer Storm
Gus's emergency plan is taped to the wall. It says "run." A storm is rolling in.
The power keeps cutting out, and the blenders only work when the lights are on.

[summer 16] Double Trouble
The volleyball team Gus used to coach won without him. They are starving.
Some of them want two smoothies.

[summer 17] VIP Visit
A famous surfer who used to ride with Gus is stopping by. His note says not to ask about the shark.
The surfer is a VIP and tips like one.

[summer 18] Summer Festival
The summer festival. Uncle Gus has come back to watch, and he is not alone.
Everything that happened this summer happens today.

[carnival 1] Carnival Day
The carnival hired you, no questions asked. Gus recommended you, which should have been a warning.
Customers marked PRIZE spin the wheel, and good smoothies in a row build a combo.

[carnival 2] Hot Blenders
The carnival's blenders were bought from a man in a van. The van was Gus's.
Blend too fast and they overheat.

[carnival 3] Belt Hiccups
The conveyor used to be a ride. Gus's note says it still remembers.
It stalls every so often.

[carnival 4] Shifty Fruit
The magician next door owes Gus a favour and won't say why. He is also bored.
Fruit on the belt keeps changing until you grab it.

[carnival 5] Short Memory
Everyone just got off the rollercoaster and forgot their own names.
Orders fade after a moment, but you can hover a customer to see theirs again.

[carnival 6] Haunted House
Your stand has been moved inside the haunted house. One of the ghosts looks a lot like Gus.
It is pitch black, so you work by flashlight.

[carnival 7] Overtime
The carnival is packed, and Gus is selling tickets he did not print.
The rush customers are back, and so are the hot blenders.

[carnival 8] Mixed Up
The magician is back, and he brought allergies.
The fruit keeps changing, and some orders have a crossed out fruit.

[carnival 9] Blackout
Gus plugged the ferris wheel into your outlet.
The power flickers and the belt stalls.

[carnival 10] Judging Day
It's the food contest. Gus entered your stand without asking.
The judges want 85% or better, and they forget their orders fast.

[carnival 11] Double Heat
The strongman contest just ended. Gus lost, somehow, to a child.
Some customers want two smoothies, and the blenders are running hot.

[carnival 12] Fortune Teller
The fortune teller says Gus is lying about something. She won't say what.
One fruit in every order is a mystery.

[carnival 13] Ice Cream Truck
The ice cream truck parked next door and froze everything out of spite. Gus says it's personal.
Frozen pieces won't turn.

[carnival 14] Hall of Mirrors
Your stand is in the hall of mirrors now. There are eleven Guses. None of them will help.
The fruit keeps changing and the orders fade.

[carnival 15] Food Fight
Someone started a food fight. Everyone is looking at Gus.
The critics want 90% or they pay nothing, and some customers want seconds.

[carnival 16] Rollercoaster
The conveyor is now technically a rollercoaster. Gus has been riding it all morning.
The belt runs faster, stalls now and then, and the rush customers love it.

[carnival 17] Rain Delay
Rain over the carnival. Gus is sheltering in your stand and eating the stock.
The power keeps cutting out, and everyone is suddenly allergic to something.

[carnival 18] Grand Finale
The last day of the carnival. Uncle Gus says he is proud of you. That is new.
Everything the carnival has thrown at you, all at once.

[tutorial]
Grab a fruit off the conveyor belt
Drop it into a blender
Right click while holding a piece to turn it || Tap with a second finger while dragging to turn a piece
Fill it to match the order, then press Blend
Drag the smoothie onto the customer
Happy customers fill the bar on the left. Reach the star goals before the shop closes!
