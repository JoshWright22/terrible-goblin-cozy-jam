# Slush Rush story text. Edit freely; the game reads this file when it starts.
#
# Each day starts with a header line: [campaign day] Title
#   campaign is "summer" (Summer Fun) or "carnival" (Boardwalk Carnival)
# The lines under it are that day's intro card. Each line starts a new line on the board.
# Long lines wrap and the text shrinks to fit; keep it to about two sentences plus the day's rule.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored. A day missing here keeps its built-in text.
#
# [tutorial] holds the Day 1 tutorial steps, one per line, in order. The turn step has a mouse
# version and a touch version split by ||

[summer 1] Opening Day
Uncle Gus left you his beach smoothie shack and fled the country. He left his journal on the counter. The first entry says the shack is completely legal.
In the back you found two unattended fruit crates.

[summer 2] New Delivery
Blueberries arrived. Nobody ordered them. Gus's journal says "The blueberries are fine. The blueberries are FINE."
A third fruit crate now sits out back.

[summer 3] Odd Shapes
The next journal entry reads "Bought a fruit cutter off a guy. It only cuts spiritual shapes. No refunds."
The fruit comes out bent, but you can right click a piece to spin it into place.

[summer 4] Mango Season
Someone carved "trust the mangoes" into the counter. It seems to be in Gus's handwriting.
A crate of mangoes turned up this morning, and the customers are craving something new.

[summer 5] Lunch Rush
A lifeguard slaps a twenty on the counter. "Ten minutes for lunch. Keep the change if you're fast."
Anyone marked RUSH won't wait long, but they pay double.

[summer 6] Allergy Season
A man hands you a laminated card listing every fruit that has tried to kill him. It is double sided.
Some orders now come with a fruit marked NO. Keep it out of the cup.

[summer 7] Rotten Batch
Flipping back through the journal, you find "Cleaned the blenders today." The entry is from two summers ago.
Old fruit has set like cement in the bottom of the blenders. You'll have to pack around it.

[summer 8] Broken Blender
The journal's cleaning page says "Hot wash, tumble dry." The old fruit came out.
The blenders came out of the dryer a size smaller. They're 3x3 now.

[summer 9] Belt on Overdrive
Page 40 of Gus's journal says "Never press the red button under the belt." That would have been good to know yesterday.
The belt now runs a lot faster than it should.

[summer 10] Heatwave
A postcard from Gus arrived. It says "hot here too." There is no return address.
Fruit left on the belt too long melts away.

[summer 11] Brain Freeze
A frosty page near the back of the journal reads "Freezer runs a little cold." It froze the fruit, the mop and a seagull. The seagull is fine.
Frozen pieces won't turn.

[summer 12] Food Critics
A food blogger found the shack and asked about Gus. You said he was on sabbatical.
Customers marked CRITIC want 90% or better, and pay triple when they get it.

[summer 13] Mystery Menu
A regular asks for her usual with "a splash" of mango. Gus's journal says "Whatever you do, don't ask how much a splash is."
One fruit in every order has no amount, just a question mark.

[summer 14] Bonfire Night
The whole beach stays out late for the bonfire, so the shack does too. Someone ran the bonfire speakers off your outlet.
The fuse blew during the first song, so you work by flashlight.

[summer 15] Summer Storm
The journal has a page titled "storms." It just says "Run." A storm is rolling in.
The power keeps cutting out, and the blenders only work when the lights are on.

[summer 16] Double Trouble
The volleyball team Gus used to coach won without him. They are starving.
Some of them want two smoothies.

[summer 17] VIP Visit
A famous surfer who used to ride with Gus is stopping by. His name is underlined twice in the journal, next to "Do not ask him about the shark."
The surfer is a VIP and tips like one.

[summer 18] Summer Festival
The summer festival. A postcard arrives with no return address. It says "Save me a smoothie." Nobody comes to collect it.
Everything that happened this summer happens today.

[carnival 1] Carnival Day
The carnival manager hired you on the spot after reading a reference letter signed "G." He also handed you a bucket of prize tickets and a wheel.
Customers marked PRIZE spin the wheel, and good smoothies in a row build a combo.

[carnival 2] Hot Blenders
The carnival's blenders came from a man in a van who asked for cash and left fast. The motors already smell like a campfire.
Blend too fast and they overheat.

[carnival 3] Belt Hiccups
Your conveyor used to be the kiddie train. It still stops every so often to let passengers off.
The belt stalls now and then.

[carnival 4] Shifty Fruit
The magician next door is practising a trick where things turn into other things. He keeps aiming it at your fruit crates.
Fruit on the belt keeps changing until you grab it.

[carnival 5] Short Memory
Your stand is at the exit of the Spin Cycle. People order, then need to lie down for a moment.
Orders fade after a moment, but you can hover a customer to see theirs again.

[carnival 6] Haunted House
Your stand has been moved inside the haunted house, which is only scary because nobody paid the light bill. One of the ghosts is wearing sandals.
It is pitch black, so you work by flashlight.

[carnival 7] Overtime
Someone sold twice as many tickets as the carnival printed. The extras are hand drawn, in handwriting you recognise from the journal.
Everyone is in a hurry, and the blenders are working hard enough to overheat.

[carnival 8] Mixed Up
The magician tried to turn a strawberry into a dove in front of a crowd. The dove came out allergic to strawberries, and so did the crowd.
The fruit is still changing, and some orders now come with a fruit marked NO.

[carnival 9] Blackout
The ferris wheel has been plugged into your outlet. The sticky note on the plug says "Borrowing this. G."
Every time the wheel turns, your power flickers and the belt stalls.

[carnival 10] Judging Day
An entry form for the food contest arrived with your stand's name on it, signed "G." The judges have been eating funnel cake since breakfast.
They want 85% or better, and they can't remember what they ordered.

[carnival 11] Double Heat
The strongman contest just ended and the competitors are starving. The winner says an old man in a Hawaiian shirt bet against her and paid in blueberries.
The strongmen want two smoothies each, and the blenders are working hard enough to overheat.

[carnival 12] Fortune Teller
The fortune teller is sending customers over with orders she read in their palms. Palms don't show amounts. She also says someone you know is lying about something.
One fruit in every order has no amount, just a question mark.

[carnival 13] Ice Cream Truck
The ice cream truck parked next door and pointed its freezer at your stand. The driver asks if you're related to Gus and doesn't wait for an answer.
Frozen pieces won't turn.

[carnival 14] Hall of Mirrors
Your stand is in the hall of mirrors now. You keep grabbing reflections of fruit instead of fruit, and one reflection is wearing a Hawaiian shirt. You aren't.
The mirrors copy your last smoothie. Serve different fruit proportions each time; repeats earn no points or health.

[carnival 15] Food Fight
Someone started a food fight at the pie stand while the critics were judging it. A blueberry stained Hawaiian shirt was found at the scene.
The critics want 90% or they pay nothing, and everyone else wants seconds to throw.

[carnival 16] Rollercoaster
The rollercoaster broke, so the operator rebuilt it around your conveyor. He says a man paid him to "make it more exciting."
The belt runs faster and stalls on the loops, and the thrill seekers won't wait long.

[carnival 17] Rain Delay
A cat came into your stand to get out of the rain. Its collar says "Property of G."
The cat tracks sticky mud into the blenders. Once fruit is placed, it stays there until you blend.

[carnival 18] Grand Finale
It's the last night of the carnival, and every act is performing at once. A postcard is waiting on the counter. It says "Proud of you. Don't look for me."
Every carnival rule returns together, including the mirrors and sticky blenders. Keep recipes varied and plan each placement.

[tutorial]
Grab a fruit off the conveyor belt
Drop it into a blender
Right click while holding a piece to turn it || Tap with a second finger while dragging to turn a piece
Fill it to match the order, then press Blend
Drag the smoothie onto the customer
Happy customers fill the bar on the left. Reach the star goals before the shop closes!
